## Consequential derived representations

When ordinary work creates a durable artifact that later action will treat as standing in for richer source material, keep that artifact distinct from its source. Retain enough explicit relation that omitted, ambiguous, or conflicting material can be reopened from the source rather than invented to satisfy the derived form. Do not treat the derived artifact as the full object by default.

This is not a per-task checklist and not required for trivial copies. It applies when the representation will govern later work.
